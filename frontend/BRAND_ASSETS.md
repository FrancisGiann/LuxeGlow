# Astrid brand icons

The existing `public/assets/astrid-nails-logo.png` remains the full salon logo used throughout the site.

The browser and home-screen icon is a simplified, blue capital A on warm ivory, derived from that supplied logo with OpenAI ImageGen on 2026-10-24. It preserves the original curled serif silhouette and palette while omitting small wording and floral detail for legibility at small sizes. It is a companion mark, not a replacement wordmark.

`public/icon-512.png` is the master icon. ImageMagick exports the 192px manifest icon, 180px Apple touch icon, 32px favicon, and ICO containing 16, 32, 48 and 64px variants. The manifest uses browser display mode and does not claim offline support.
